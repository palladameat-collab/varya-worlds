Effective date: May 3, 2026 · Last updated: May 3, 2026

1. Who we are

Varya Worlds ("we", "our", "us") is an independent media channel publishing animated and family content on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and Bilibili. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you interact with our website and our official accounts.

2. Information we collect

We do not run analytics, tracking, or advertising scripts on this website. The site is a static landing page only.

When you contact us by email at palladameat@gmail.com, we receive whatever information you choose to send (your email address and message contents). We use this information only to reply to your message.

If you interact with our content on third-party platforms (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Bilibili), those platforms collect data according to their own privacy policies. We receive only aggregated, non-identifying analytics provided by those platforms.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to messages you send us.
  • To improve our content based on aggregated platform analytics.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing.

4. Children's privacy

Our content is family-friendly and intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

This website does not set cookies.
